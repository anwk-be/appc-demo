Who can join

Postal operators in Asia and the Pacific

Membership is open to designated postal operators in the Asia Pacific region. If you are unsure whether your organisation qualifies, the secretariat is happy to talk it through.

  • Designated postal operators in an Asia Pacific country or territory
  • Committed to taking part in the General Assembly and programmes
  • Able to pay the annual membership contribution

Questions

How much does membership cost?

Contributions are set by the General Assembly. The secretariat will share current rates when you enquire.

How long does joining take?

Typically a few months, depending on the Board meeting calendar.

Who in our organisation can use the member community?

Any staff your post nominates, from executives to working-level teams.
