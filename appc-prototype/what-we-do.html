Programme spotlight

Quality of service and QSF support

Our team guides postal operators through the UPU Quality of Service Fund framework. We help members assess their performance, plan improvements and prepare Common Fund applications that are more likely to be funded.

  • Performance reviews against UPU targets
  • Project design and application drafting
  • Online clinics for member staff

Cross-border e-commerce

The APP Cross Border Passport is our guide to Asia Pacific e-commerce for postal professionals. Alongside it, ePacket is an award-winning product focused on quality of service for lightweight cross-border parcels.
