APPC secretariat to move to Bangkok in 2027

Announcement28 October 20263 min read

Sample article written for the prototype. Replace with the secretariat's approved text.

After three decades in Singapore, the APPC secretariat will move to Bangkok, Thailand, in 2027.

The move was approved by members at the 2026 General Assembly. Bangkok sits close to the centre of our network, with direct flights to most member capitals and a strong community of regional postal and logistics organisations.

"Moving to Bangkok puts the secretariat closer to the people we serve and the partners we work with."

What changes for members

Very little day to day. Our email address, the member community and all programmes continue as normal. We will share the new office address and a timeline for the move in early 2027.

What happens next

  • Early 2027: new office address confirmed
  • Mid 2027: secretariat begins operating from Bangkok
  • June 2027: first General Assembly hosted from the new office

Questions about the move? Contact the secretariat.
