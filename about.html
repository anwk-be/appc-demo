About APPC

Since 1994, the Asia Pacific Post Cooperative has brought the region's postal operators together to serve their customers better.

Our mission

Give every member post the tools to grow

We equip our members with tools and resources that protect and grow their place in the market.

APPC is a hub that connects postal operators, suppliers, NGOs and other stakeholders across Asia and the Pacific. Together, our members are the daily communications backbone for about 4.5 billion people.

  • Raise quality of service across the network
  • Make cross-border e-commerce simpler for customers
  • Share knowledge, data and people between posts
Our history

Three decades of cooperation

  1. 1994Postal operators across Asia and the Pacific found the cooperative to work together on shared challenges.
  2. 2000sMembership grows across East, South and Southeast Asia, Oceania and the Pacific Islands.
  3. 2010sePacket and quality programmes help members grow cross-border e-commerce.
  4. Today28 member posts, a Management Board elected by the General Assembly and a small secretariat.
  5. 2027The secretariat moves from Singapore to Bangkok, Thailand.
